# Support

**English** · [Русский](SUPPORT_ru.md) · [中文](SUPPORT_zh.md)

Work on an open internet is not free. Sponsorship is accepted so that the protocol, the node, and the clients may be kept independent.

Loft is licensed Apache License 2.0. loft/1.0 may be implemented from the specification without a fee. The reference node, the macOS application, the CLI, and the remainder of this tree are built without a company budget.

ETH and USDT ERC-20 are received at the same Ethereum address. TRX and USDT TRC20 are received at the same TRON address. USDT must be sent on the network of that row. BSC, TON, and other USDT contracts use different addresses; those transfers do not arrive.

On-chain fees are paid by the sender. A small USDT transfer on Ethereum may be smaller than the gas. There is no minimum. A transfer does not buy invoices, membership, or extra protocol rights.
